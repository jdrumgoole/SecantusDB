### Rust PostgreSQL server: ICU collation data for seven more languages

ICU4X's compiled collation data has no tailoring for Hawaiian,
Greenlandic, Lakota, Northern and Inari Sami or Yiddish, and a different
one for Lingala, so PostgreSQL's collations for them ordered as the root
here and were refused. Their data is now generated from ICU's own release.

#### Added

- **`haw`, `kl`, `lkt`, `ln`, `se`, `smn` and `yi` ICU collations**, with
  every regional form (19 collations), ordering as PostgreSQL 15.19 does.
  `pg_collation` lists 897 of PostgreSQL's 907, each checked over a probe
  of 278 strings across 25 scripts. Part of RP-005
  (tests/backlog/test_rp_triage.py::test_tailored_icu_collation_orders_as_postgres).
  The data is `icu4x-datagen`'s baked Rust source in
  `crates/secantus-pgplan/src/icu_baked/`, regenerated by
  `scripts/icu_collation_data.sh`; every other locale keeps ICU4X's
  compiled data.

`mr`, `as` and `kok` stay refused: ICU4X does not apply their conjunct
rule, with this data or without.
