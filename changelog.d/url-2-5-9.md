### `secantusd-mdb` builds against `url` 2.5.9

The standalone MongoDB server's lockfile moves the `url` crate from 2.5.8
to 2.5.9 (and `form_urlencoded` from 1.2.2 to 1.2.3), clearing
RUSTSEC-2026-0592, a stack overflow in `Url::origin()` on nested `blob:`
URLs, which `cargo audit` began reporting on 2026-11-20.

#### Security

- `crates/secantusdb/Cargo.lock`: `url` 2.5.9, `form_urlencoded` 1.2.3
  (RUSTSEC-2026-0592).
