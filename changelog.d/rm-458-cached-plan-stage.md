### A find that runs the plan cache's plan reports its CACHED_PLAN stage

When mongod's plan cache holds an active plan for a query that two indexes could answer, mongod runs that plan in place of a fresh trial. It runs it under a CACHED_PLAN stage, which first hands out the results of the plan's short trial, one per call, and then passes on every result the plan reads after it. The profile entries of the `find` and of each `getMore` carry that stage over the plan's own stages, counting what it has handed out so far. The Rust MongoDB server's entries started at the plan's FETCH, so a tool that read the profile could not tell a cached plan from a freshly tried one.

The entries now carry the CACHED_PLAN with mongod 8.2.11's counts, under a limit, a skip, a sort and any batch size. When the trial reaches the plan's end or fills its limit, mongod's stage notices without working the plan again, so the find's last work belongs to the CACHED_PLAN and not to the stage under it. This server now counts that work the same way. A query that mongod plans by a fresh trial, or that it replans, still carries no stage above the winner's, on mongod and here alike.

#### Fixed

- `secantus-commands` (`explain/plan.rs`, `explain/order.rs`, `explain/stream.rs`, `find.rs`): the planner records when the plan cache's active plan ran in place of a trial (`Trial::cached_ran`), and `TrialBuffer::cached` passes that to the stream. `Stream::buffer_trial` then keeps the CACHED_PLAN's state: its trial results past the skip, how many are still to hand out, and the plan's works and needTime when the trial stopped. A trial that ran out or filled its LIMIT leaves the find's last work to the CACHED_PLAN. `Stream::exec_stats` puts the stage over the plan's stages. Fixes RM-458 (tests/backlog/test_rm_355_364.py::test_a_read_of_the_cached_plan_reports_its_cached_plan_stage).
- Regression test: `tests/backlog/test_rm_355_364.py::test_a_cached_read_reports_its_cached_plan_as_it_ran` runs the seven contested shapes and five more with an active cache entry: a trial that ran out, a LIMIT filled after the trial in the first batch and in a `getMore`, a SKIP under a LIMIT, and a first batch that reads to the end. Each entry's counts and `execStats` are compared with mongod's.
