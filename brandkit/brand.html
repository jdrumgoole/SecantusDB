Usage notes

Clear space
Maintain padding equal to the height of the "S" in "Secantus" on all sides of any lockup.
Minimum size
Mark: 24px. Horizontal lockup with tagline: 220px wide. Below that, drop the tagline; below 160px, drop to the mark only.
Don't
Recolour the cyan to other hues. Skew or rotate. Place over busy photos without an overlay. Re-set the wordmark in a different typeface.
Do
Use the SVGs directly — no rasterisation needed for web. Inline the mark in your HTML for theme-aware currentColor tricks if you want.
