## 🔴 CRITICAL (Immediate action required)

### New this cycle

- **Unbounded, uninterruptible CPU blowup via `explain` of a
  `$planCacheStats` pipeline — no collection, auth, or stage-count gate**
  (high/high/high).
  A database-level `explain` of `{aggregate: 1, pipeline: [{$planCacheStats:
  {}}, ...]}` reaches `own_source_reply`
  (`crates/secantus-commands/src/explain/mod.rs`), which unconditionally
  calls the new `plan_cache::absorb_matches()`
  (`crates/secantus-commands/src/explain/plan_cache.rs:400-440`) regardless
  of requested explain verbosity. `absorb_matches()` calls the pre-existing
  `hoist_after_source()`/`hoist()`
  (`crates/secantus-commands/src/explain/hoist.rs:608-756`) once per trailing
  `$match` stage, over a "remaining stages" slice whose non-match prefix
  never shrinks between calls. `hoist()`'s own `'again: loop` recomputes its
  leading-prefix predicate from scratch at every position tried
  (`hoist.rs:632-636`) and restarts the whole scan from position 1 after
  every single-position relocation (five `continue 'again` sites), making
  one `hoist_after_source` call itself cubic in the number of leading stages
  crossed; `absorb_matches` then pays that cost once per trailing `$match`
  without the leading-stage count ever shrinking, giving a total cost of at
  least Θ(n³) and plausibly Θ(n⁴) in the pipeline's total stage count. No
  collection or stored document is required (the collection-existence check
  at `mod.rs:2736` does not fire for a database-level `{aggregate: 1, ...}`
  target), it fires even at the cheapest `queryPlanner` verbosity (the call
  at `mod.rs:2776` is gated only on the pipeline's first stage being
  `$planCacheStats`, not on the verbosity flag), and no `maxTimeMS`/interrupt
  check exists anywhere on this call path, unlike ordinary query-plan
  generation (`explain/plan.rs:101`). A pipeline of a few hundred to a few
  thousand trivial stages — well under 1 MB of BSON, far below the 16 MB
  document cap — is plausible to pin one CPU core for seconds to minutes,
  with no way for the client or the operator's `maxTimeMS`/`killOp` to stop
  it once started; a handful of connections (comparable to the host's core
  count) denies service to every other client. An independent verifier
  re-derived every component of this claim from source, including
  discovering that the true asymptotic cost is *worse* than the hunter's own
  cubic estimate (an additional unmemoized prefix rescan inside `hoist()`'s
  loop compounds with the restart-on-relocation pattern), and confirmed no
  cap of any kind exists on the call path.
  **Fix:** make `hoist()`'s leading-prefix test incremental instead of
  recomputed from scratch at every position, and resume its scan from just
  behind a relocation instead of restarting from position 1; add a periodic
  `secantus_core::deadline::check_for_interrupt()` poll inside `hoist()`/
  `absorb_matches()` (mirroring `explain/plan.rs`'s existing pattern) so
  `maxTimeMS`/`killOp` can bound it; consider a pipeline-stage-count cap as
  defense in depth, matching mongod's own `internalPipelineLengthLimit`.
  **Filed as a new issue**, cross-referencing the related-but-distinct,
  already-open **#2541** (same root-cause shape in `hoist.rs`, different,
  pre-existing call site), not a duplicate of it.
