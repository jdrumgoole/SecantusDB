## Building SecantusDB

**PyCon Ireland 2026** · Joe Drumgoole · 30 minutes

Two Rust database servers that speak the MongoDB and PostgreSQL wire
protocols over WiredTiger, the storage engine MongoDB ships, and the Python
prototype that got us there. The talk covers why the first version was
written in Python, how every behaviour is measured against the real
`mongod` and PostgreSQL rather than our own code, the move to Rust and a
second protocol, and how the project treats errors, its backlog and working
with AI agents.
