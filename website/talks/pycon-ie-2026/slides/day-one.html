Scope: what does pymongo actually send?

26 April 2026: the first commit, a MongoDB wire server in Python. The next day it had sort, projection and cursor pagination through getMore.

An asyncio socket server, OP_MSG parsing, BSON in and out.

The scope came from one question: what does pymongo actually send? The handshake, CRUD, cursors, aggregation, findAndModify, and then change streams.

Python was the right first language. It was the fastest way to learn the protocol, and pymongo is the client we target. But it was a prototype; the product is the Rust servers.

Time: ~3 min
