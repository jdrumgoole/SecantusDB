Scope: what does pymongo actually send?

26 April 2026: the first commit, a MongoDB wire server in Python: an asyncio socket server, OP_MSG parsing, BSON in and out. The next day it had sort, projection and cursor pagination through getMore.

The scope came from one question: what does pymongo actually send? The handshake, CRUD, cursors, aggregation, findAndModify, then change streams.

Python was the fastest way to learn the protocol, and pymongo is the client we target.

Time: ~1½ min
