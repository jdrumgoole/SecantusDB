Why build a database?

Two protocols everyone already speaks

The clients exist

pymongo, psycopg, SQLAlchemy and drivers in a dozen languages. Speak their wire protocol and they all work.

The engine exists

WiredTiger, the storage engine inside MongoDB: checkpoints, transactions, durability.

The gap

One small, self-contained server that speaks both, starts in one line and needs nothing else installed.

Set up the motivation: we didn’t have to invent a client ecosystem or a storage engine. The work is the server in between, and being faithful to the real products. ~2 min.
