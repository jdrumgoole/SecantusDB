The idea

A real database. Not a toy.

Real wire protocols. Real storage. Single node.

Clients connect over TCP, and for what we support they can't tell us apart from mongod or PostgreSQL. Data is durable on disk, in WiredTiger.

Deliberately single-node: no replica sets, no sharding. It's a beta, so no production claims, but it's a real database.

Time: ~45 s
