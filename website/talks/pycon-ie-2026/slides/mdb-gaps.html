Act two · The gaps

What secantusd-mdb doesn’t do

Not supported

  • Server-side JavaScript: $where, $function, $accumulator, mapReduce
  • Text search: text indexes and $text
  • Hashed indexes
  • Queryable Encryption collections
  • Auth beyond SCRAM and X.509: no LDAP, Kerberos, AWS IAM, OIDC

Out of scope: one node, by design

  • Replica sets with real members: no secondaries, no elections
  • Sharding: no mongos, no config servers
  • Atlas Search and Vector Search: Atlas-only, as on mongod

Checked against mongod 8.2.11 and secantusd-mdb 0.5.3-beta.198 · 19 November 2026

Be explicit about the gaps; every one is a clean, named error, never a silent wrong answer.

Not supported: anything that runs JavaScript on the server, since we ship no JS runtime (MongoDB itself deprecated $where and mapReduce); full-text search and text indexes; hashed indexes; Queryable Encryption; and authentication beyond SCRAM and X.509.

Out of scope by design: it is a single-node server. It reports itself as a one-member replica set so change streams work, but there are no secondaries and no elections, and no sharding. Atlas Search and Vector Search are Atlas-only; a plain mongod refuses them the same way.

If asked what does work: wildcard indexes, $median and $percentile, time-series, capped and clustered collections, geo with $geoNear, $merge, views, transactions, change streams and the 8.0 bulkWrite command.
