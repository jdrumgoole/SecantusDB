The single most important early decision.

WiredTiger holds the bytes. All filtering, projection, sorting and updates happen in the engine after decoding; the storage layer never inspects document content.

Why: any lossy middle step breaks fidelity. Round-trip an Int64 through JSON and it comes back as an int32, and pymongo can tell. The same goes for ObjectId, Decimal128, dates with time zones, Binary and Regex.

Indexes are WiredTiger indexes over typed sort-key columns derived from the BSON values, never coerced numbers.

Using the engine mongod ships gives us real durability, checkpoints and transactions instead of a dict.

Time: ~2 min
