The critical decision

Store documents as opaque BSON blobs

WiredTiger holds the bytes. Filtering, projection, sorting and updates happen in the engine, after decoding.

No JSON, no native column types: any lossy middle step breaks ObjectId, Decimal128, int32 vs int64, dates, Binary, Regex.

Indexes are WiredTiger indexes over typed sort-key columns — never coerced numbers.

# a JSON round trip
{"n": Int64(1)}
→ {"n": 1}
→ int32

pymongo can tell.
# so we never do it
The single most important early decision. Using WiredTiger — the engine mongod itself ships — gives us real durability, checkpoints and transactions instead of a dict. ~2 min.
