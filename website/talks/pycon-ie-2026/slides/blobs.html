The critical decision

Store documents as opaque BSON blobs

WiredTiger holds the bytes. No lossy middle step.

# a JSON round trip
{"n": Int64(1)}
→ {"n": 1}
→ int32

pymongo can tell.
# so we never do it

The single most important early decision. WiredTiger holds the bytes; filtering, projection, sorting and updates all happen in the engine after decoding.

Any lossy middle step breaks fidelity. Round-trip an Int64 through JSON and it comes back as an int32, and pymongo can tell. Same for ObjectId, Decimal128, dates, Binary and Regex.

Indexes are WiredTiger indexes over typed sort keys derived from the BSON values, never coerced numbers.

Time: ~1½ min
