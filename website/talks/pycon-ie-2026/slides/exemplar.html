Act two · How do you know it’s right?

Run it against the real server. Don’t reason about the source.

One exemplar

mongod 8.x and PostgreSQL. Never another SecantusDB server, never our own docs.

Differential probes

Same pymongo code against SecantusDB and real mongod; diff the answers, including BSON types.

Parity isn’t correctness

Our Python and Rust servers once agreed on $bucket, $densify and $stdDev — and were both wrong.

Every real bug in our August sweeps came from executing behaviour and comparing, not from reading code. Estimates from reading code were wrong in both directions. Tell the $asinh story briefly: two of our own engines agreed, mongod disagreed. ~3 min.
