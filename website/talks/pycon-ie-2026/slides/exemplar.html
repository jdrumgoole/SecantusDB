Parity isn't correctness. The $asinh story, September 2026: our Python and Rust engines disagreed on very small inputs. The Python answer looked mathematically right, so we changed Rust to match, and that moved it away from mongod, which underflows to 0 below 1E-4966. The engine-against-engine sweep then said “0 divergent of 384” and we cited it as proof. A sweep against mongod alone, 1,298 cases, found three shapes the other sweep had been blind to.
