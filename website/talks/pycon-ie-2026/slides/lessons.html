What I’d tell you before you build one

  • Pick one exemplar and measure against it. Never against yourself.
  • Make the representation lossless on day one. You can’t retrofit fidelity.
  • Prototype in Python, then rewrite once you know the design.
  • Steal test suites. Driver authors already wrote your spec.
  • Write the rules down — for humans and for agents.
