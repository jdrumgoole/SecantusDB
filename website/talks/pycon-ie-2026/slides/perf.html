Latency as a multiple of mongod’s, lower is better · both durable · 18 November 2026

Per-operation latency as a multiple of mongod 8.0.38's: the median of 15 interleaved repetitions on a DigitalOcean droplet, 18 November 2026. Both run as a single-node replica set, so a default write waits for the journal on both.

The same WiredTiger library sits under both, so every gap is in the layers above storage. Reads, update_many and the change-stream drain are within 1.2–1.4×; delete_many (3.6×) and aggregation (2.6–2.8×) are where the work is.

"(half)": each workload runs over 10,000 documents, every second one with active: true. update_many (half) is one update_many({active: true}, {$inc: {v: 1}}), updating 5,000 documents in one command; delete_many (half) is one delete_many({active: false}), deleting the other 5,000. So those two bars are one multi-document write over half the collection, not a single-document update or delete.

If asked: the benchmark ran against 8.0.38; correctness is measured against 8.2.

Time: ~1½ min
