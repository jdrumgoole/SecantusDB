The idea

A real database. Not a toy.

A real server on a real TCP socket, speaking the real wire protocols, storing data in WiredTiger — deliberately single-node.

The key framing. This is not an in-memory mock pretending to be MongoDB: clients connect over TCP and, for what we support, cannot tell the difference from mongod or PostgreSQL. Data is durable on disk. Out of scope by design: replica sets, sharding, replication. ~1 min.
