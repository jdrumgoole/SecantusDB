The idea

A real database. Not a toy.

Real wire protocols. Real storage. Single node.

The key framing. This is not an in-memory mock pretending to be MongoDB.

Clients connect over TCP, and for what we support they cannot tell the difference from mongod or PostgreSQL. Data is durable on disk, in WiredTiger.

It is deliberately single-node. Replica sets, sharding and replication are out of scope by design. The MongoDB server reports itself as a one-member replica-set primary, so change streams work, but there are no other members.

It's a beta: no production-proven or SLA claims. But it's a real database, and tests are one use among many, not the pitch.
