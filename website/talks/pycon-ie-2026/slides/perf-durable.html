Benchmark lesson: a standalone mongod doesn't wait for the journal. Compare like with like, or you are measuring durability, not speed.

The 0.14× looked like a huge regression. It wasn't: our server, as a replica-set primary, now waits for the journal on every default write, which is what mongod does in the same mode. The benchmark was comparing against a standalone mongod that never waits. Put mongod in the same mode and it loses about half its throughput, and the ratio is 0.51×. ~2 min.
